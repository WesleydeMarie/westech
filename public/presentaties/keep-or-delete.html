02 · The law

The law pulls both ways

Kept too short

You breach a retention duty

Dutch tax law requires 7 years, 10 years for real estate records. Public bodies also have to follow the Archives Act.

Kept too long

You breach the GDPR

Personal data may not be kept longer than necessary. Every extra year makes a breach bigger and a freedom of information request or eDiscovery case heavier.

Keep it as long as you must, delete it as soon as you may

03 · Policy or label

Two ways to retain

Retention policy

For a whole location

Covers everything in a site, mailbox or Teams location. Several policies can apply to the same item.

A good safety net for the whole organization.

Retention label

For one item

Travels with the item when it moves within the tenant. Only a label can start the period at an event, mark an item as a record and require a disposition review.

04 · Year 1

Gone for the user, not for Purview

A user cleans up and deletes the contract. For them it is gone. Because the label still applies, Purview keeps a copy in a secured location.

Preservation Hold library
Retention wins

SharePoint and OneDrive keep the copy in the Preservation Hold library, Exchange in Recoverable Items. That library counts toward the site storage quota.

08 · Dutch public sector
