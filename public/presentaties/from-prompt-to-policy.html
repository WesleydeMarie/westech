06 · Protect

Four routes per site

Restrict access by label

DLP stops Copilot from summarising labeled items.

Restrict all items

Restricted Content Discovery excludes the whole site from Copilot.

Auto-labeling

A policy labels unlabeled files that contain sensitive data.

Retention

Content not opened for three years is deleted automatically.

07 · One-click policies

Look first, then block

Discover

Audit mode

Detect sensitive info added to AI sites sees what is pasted or uploaded to AI sites, without stopping anything.

Protect

Block

Protect sensitive data from Copilot processing keeps Copilot away from chosen labels. Block sensitive info from AI sites gives elevated risk a block with override, in test mode.

Policies using Adaptive Protection turn Adaptive Protection on if it wasn't already, with default risk levels for all users and groups.
