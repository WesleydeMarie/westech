02 · The case

Everything starts with a case

CaseMembers
Case

The central unit

Searches, holds and review sets belong to a case. You decide who is a member. Content Search still exists as a system-generated case.

By default the Content Search case is visible to all members of eDiscovery Manager and Administrator. Restricting it is up to you, in case settings.

03 · Data sources

Pick a person, eDiscovery finds the locations

You add a user or group. eDiscovery finds the related mailbox, OneDrive and sites. Then you refine what does and doesn't belong.

04 · Day 1 · Hold

Preserve first, then search

Hold

Whole location

Everything in the mailbox or site is preserved, whatever happens.

Hold

Query-based hold

Only what matches a query is preserved.

A hold is optional, but while it's in place nothing is permanently removed. It doesn't go away by itself either: you release it.
