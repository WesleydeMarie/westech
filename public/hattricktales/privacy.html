Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the Hat Trick Tales website pages and the Hat Trick Tales Publisher tool, which the channel owner uses to upload videos to the Hat Trick Tales accounts on YouTube and TikTok.

What data the tool uses

  • Account access tokens from Google (YouTube Data API) and TikTok (Login Kit / Content Posting API), granted by the channel owner when logging in.
  • Basic account information needed to check that videos go to the right channel (channel or account name and ID).
  • Our own video files, titles and descriptions, which we upload.

How the data is used

Only to upload, schedule and manage videos, thumbnails and descriptions on the Hat Trick Tales accounts. We do not read, collect or store data about viewers or other users, we do not use the data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with anyone.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on a private, non-public server owned by the channel owner, in files readable only by the publishing tool, and are never published or sent to third parties other than Google and TikTok themselves.

Google API Services

Hat Trick Tales Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time via Google account permissions or in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Apps and services). On revocation or on request we delete the stored tokens within 7 days.

Website

These pages use no cookies and no tracking.
